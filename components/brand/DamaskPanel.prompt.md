Full-bleed purple damask section — the page's anchor block.

```jsx
<DamaskPanel contrast={0.45}>
  <Eyebrow tone="lilac">Three ways to use it</Eyebrow>
  <h2 style={{ color: "var(--white)" }}>One masque, three routines.</h2>
</DamaskPanel>
```

Two or three per page maximum. All text on it is white; secondary text is lilac.
